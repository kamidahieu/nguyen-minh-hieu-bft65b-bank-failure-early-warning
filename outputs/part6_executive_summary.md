# Part 6: Executive Conclusions — Answers to Q1–Q3, Limitations & SBV Adaptation

## 1. Evidence-Based Answers to Research Questions (Q1–Q3)

### Q1. How should four decades of bank reports be stored and processed on a laptop, and when would a Spark cluster be needed?
- **Storage Layout (`Part 1`):** Storing the 42-year FDIC Call Report panel (`1984Q1–2026Q2`, `170` quarters, `1,682,738` bank-quarters, `24,064` distinct `CERT` institutions, `33` columns) as quarter-partitioned Parquet (`REPDTE=YYYYMMDD.parquet`) with **Snappy** compression reduces the disk footprint from **422.52 MB** (uncompressed CSV) to **236.76 MB** (**1.78x** compression). Combined with Polars lazy scanning (`pl.scan_parquet`), full-table scan latency drops from **3.0096 s** (pandas CSV) to **0.1395 s** (**21.6x** speedup), and a filtered 6-column post-2008 query drops from **0.9201 s** to **0.0908 s** (**10.13x** speedup) via partition pruning and columnar projection pushdown.
- **Single-Node Processing (`Part 3`):** On the `1,664,114`-row modeling panel, computing 16 panel transformations (ratios, 1Q/4Q lags, 4Q rolling means/volatilities per contiguous bank spell, and cross-sectional quarter demeaning) takes **0.2536 s** in Polars Lazy (parallel) and **0.3087 s** in Polars Streaming (**32.34x** and **26.56x** faster than pandas at **8.2012 s**, and **22.2x** faster than local PySpark at **5.6398 s**), with exact numerical equivalence (`max_abs_diff <= 7.11e-15` between Polars and PySpark across all 16 features).
- **When a Multi-Node Spark Cluster Is Justified:** Our empirical scaling experiment (`0.25x` to `4.0x`, up to `6.66M` rows) shows Polars scaling linearly at `~6.2M rows/second` (**1.067 s** for `6.66M` rows) with `~350 MB` working memory per `1M` rows. A laptop/workstation with 32–64 GB RAM handles `50M–100M` rows in-memory (or `>200M` rows via Polars streaming) without the JVM serialization (`2.11 GB` RSS) and two network shuffles (`Exchange hashpartitioning`) observed in Spark's physical plan. A multi-node Spark cluster is justified only when working dataset sizes exceed single-node memory (`>50–100 GB` uncompressed / `>150M–300M` rows, such as loan-level credit registry or daily payment settlement flows) or when pipelines must execute inside an existing distributed lakehouse (HDFS/S3/Delta Lake).

### Q2. What quality problems do the raw data contain, and how does the pipeline handle them without look-ahead bias?
- **Systematic Audit Findings (`Part 2`):**
  1. **Cross-Partition Schema Drift:** pandas serializes integer Call Report columns without nulls as `Int64` in some quarters and `Float64` when nulls appear in others (e.g., `LNCON`). Handled via an explicit canonical schema (`Float64` for all 30 financial fields, `Int64` for `CERT`, `String` for `REPDTE`/`STNAME`).
  2. **Regulatory Sentinel Zeros in Capital Ratios:** `RBCRWAJ` (Tier 1 risk-based capital ratio) has `0` formal nulls but `468,480` zeros (`27.8%` of all rows): **100.0% of 1980s rows** (pre-Basel I, 1984–1989) and **36.92% of 2020s rows** (2020+ Community Bank Leverage Ratio / CBLR opt-in exemption) are recorded as `0.0` despite non-zero equity (`EQ != 0`). Similarly, `RBC1AAJ` is `0.0` for **17.97% of 1980s rows** (FSLIC thrifts). Left untreated, these sentinel zeros falsely portray healthy 2020s CBLR community banks as capital-depleted. Handled point-in-time at row $(i,q)$ by recoding `RBCRWAJ == 0` and `RBC1AAJ == 0` (when `EQ != 0`) to `null` and recording binary structural indicator flags (`flag_rbcrwaj_struct_zero`, `flag_rbc1aaj_struct_zero`).
  3. **Era-Specific Schedule Confidentiality:** `P3ASSET` (30–89 day past-due assets) is **100.0% null from 1984 to 2000** (`57.8%` of panel) because early delinquency was confidential prior to 2001Q1. Handled via `flag_p3asset_missing` and `SimpleImputer(add_indicator=True, keep_empty_features=True)`.
  4. **Valid Insolvency/Loss Negatives vs. Accounting Errors:** `EQ < 0` (`15,647` rows, `0.93%`) and `NETINC < 0` (`172,749` rows, `10.27%`) are genuine distress signals and preserved (`flag_negative_equity`), whereas `989` rows with impossible negative balance-sheet stocks/gross flows (e.g., negative `ORE`, `SC`, `LNLSGR`) are flagged (`flag_impossible_neg`) and set to `null` at row $(i,q)$.
  5. **Discontinuous Reporting Spells & Exit Reconciliation:** Across `24,064` banks, `33` reporting gaps (`q_step > 1` quarter) occur across `32` banks. Mergers/voluntary closures (`16,345` banks via `CHANGEC1` codes `223`, `211`, `221`, etc.) are labeled $y_{i,q} = 0$. Crucially, `1,095` Call Reports filed *on or after* an institution's outright failure date (`rep_dt >= fail_dt`, primarily 1985–1992 S&Ls in FSLIC/RTC conservatorship) are excluded so already-failed institutions are not mislabeled as healthy ($y_{i,q}=0$).
- **Zero Look-Ahead Guarantee (`Part 4`):** Causal 1-quarter forward-filling and 1Q/4Q lags/rolling windows operate strictly within contiguous `(CERT, spell_id)` reporting spells. Truncating the raw panel at `2006Q4` yields **`max_abs_diff = 0.0`** across all 39 CAMELS features on Development, and all clipping quantiles, imputation medians, scalers, and alarm thresholds are fitted exclusively on `dev` (`<= 2006Q4`).

### Q3. Can the pipeline's output support an honestly validated early-warning model?
- **Yes (`Part 5`):** Using a strict 4-quarter embargo gap (`2007Q1–2007Q4`) between Development (`1984Q1–2006Q4`: `1,193,914` rows, `2,658` failing banks) and the single held-out Test evaluation (`2008Q1–2025Q2`: `435,446` rows, `558` failing banks, `2,181` positive bank-quarters), `HistGradientBoosting (GBM)` achieves:
  - **Test PR-AUC = `0.6161`** (`123x` lift over the `0.501%` base rate) and **Test ROC-AUC = `0.9834`** (`Brier = 0.00313`).
  - **Supervisory Alert Budget Capture:** **77.35%** of all 4-quarter ahead failures are captured in the **Top-1%** highest-risk bank-quarters (`93.76%` in the Top-5%).
  - **Data-Driven Threshold Performance:** Using the frozen Development threshold $\tau^*_{\text{dev}} = 0.0180$ (derived from the empirical Development median DIF resolution cost of \$9.60M per failed bank vs. targeted examination costs, $C_{\text{FN}}:C_{\text{FP}} = 20:1$), the model captures **84.69% of Test failures** at **28.82% precision**, reducing expected total supervisory + DIF loss costs by **74.23%** (\$776.98M net savings on Test).

## 2. Limitations & What Would Change for Vietnam (State Bank of Vietnam / SBV)

### Key Limitations Identified Empirically
1. **Regime Shift from Credit Solvency to Liquidity/Duration Runs (`2008 GFC` vs. `2023 Regional Bank Crisis`):** Our Test sub-period breakdown (`outputs/part5_test_subperiods.csv`) shows that while GBM achieves **PR-AUC = `0.6466`** (`ROC-AUC = 0.9823`, `85.87%` recall) during the credit-driven 2008–2014 GFC, PR-AUC drops to **`0.0762`** (`0.1901` for L2 Logistic Regression, though `ROC-AUC` remains `0.9101` and `Recall@Top-1%` is `45.45%`) during `2023Q1–2025Q2`. The 2023 regional bank failures (SVB, Signature, First Republic) were driven by **uninsured deposit flight** and **unrealized Held-to-Maturity (HTM) securities duration losses** following rapid interest-rate hikes—variables that are not fully captured by traditional credit-delinquency ratios (`texas_ratio`, `npl_to_loans`).
2. **Quarterly Reporting Lag & Accounting Discretion:** Quarterly Call Reports arrive with a 30–45 day filing lag and rely on self-reported loan loss provisions (`LNATRES`, `ELNATR`) and nonaccrual classifications (`NAASSET`), which distressed banks may delay recognizing ("evergreening" / forbearance).
3. **Macroeconomic & Contagion Omission:** Bank-level Call Report ratios treat institutions as conditionally independent given their balance sheets, omitting interbank exposure networks, regional real-estate price indices, and sovereign yield-curve shifts.

### Architectural & Modeling Adaptations for the State Bank of Vietnam (SBV)
1. **Different Institutional & Resolution Regime:** Unlike the US (~4,300–14,000 institutions with frequent formal FDIC receiverships), Vietnam's banking sector comprises ~35–49 commercial banks (State-Owned Commercial Banks, Joint-Stock Commercial Banks, Joint-Venture/100% Foreign-Owned Banks) plus ~1,200 People's Credit Funds (*Quỹ tín dụng nhân dân*, QTDND). Formal commercial bank liquidation is historically rare in Vietnam; instead, distress manifests as **SBV "Special Control" (*Kiểm soát đặc biệt*), mandatory zero-dong acquisition/restructuring** (e.g., OceanBank, GPBank, VNCB/CB, DongA Bank, SCB), or emergency liquidity support.
   - **Label Adaptation for SBV:** Redefine $y_{i,q} = 1$ from outright closure to a composite supervisory distress event: entry into SBV Special Control, regulatory capital adequacy ratio (CAR under SBV Circular 41/2016/TT-NHNN Basel II) falling below regulatory thresholds, emergency SBV refinancing/special loans, or forced restructuring.
2. **Handling VAMC Special Bonds & Restructured Loans:** In Vietnam, nonperforming loans transferred to the Vietnam Asset Management Company (VAMC) in exchange for VAMC special bonds or restructured under temporary forbearance circulars (e.g., Circular 01/2020, Circular 02/2023) can mask true asset quality in headline NPL ratios (Group 3–5 loans). The SBV pipeline must integrate **CIC (Credit Information Center) borrower-level cross-bank debt group classifications**, VAMC special bond balances, and accrued interest receivable (*Lãi dự thu* / `INTINC` vs. actual cash interest collected) to detect hidden credit deterioration.
3. **High-Frequency Liquidity & Related-Party Monitoring:** Because Vietnam's commercial bank panel is compact (~50 banks) while credit-registry and interbank data are large, SBV should combine Polars for quarterly/monthly bank-level panel analytics with a central data lake integrating daily CITAD/IBPS interbank payment flows, bond-market/real-estate group cross-ownership concentration, and deposit maturity mismatches.
